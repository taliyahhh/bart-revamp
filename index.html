Karl

the Fog

Since 2010

Who is Karl?

Karl is the name the Bay Area gave its famous fog, the thick marine layer that pours through the Golden Gate, swallows the bridge towers and turns a sunny morning into a gray afternoon in about ten minutes.

Why "Karl"?

In August 2010 an anonymous San Franciscan started a social media account speaking as the fog, to stick up for it after friends kept complaining. The name comes from Karl, the giant in the 2003 film Big Fish: feared by everyone, but really just misunderstood.

How he shows up

When inland valleys heat up, warm air rises and pulls cool, damp ocean air toward the Bay. The cold water off the coast condenses it into fog, which is why Karl is busiest in summer, exactly when visitors pack shorts.

Karl's friend

Karl hangs out above the water. Bart runs 135 feet below it, through the Transbay Tube. Two Bay Area icons, two very different commutes, and the reason this site has its name.
